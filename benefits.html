Section V — M&E

Monitoring & Evaluation Framework

How the project will stay accountable to its KPIs once implementation begins

01

Monthly Progress Reports

Aligned with Ongoing Monitoring
  • Monthly Updates. Project leads submit monthly updates on enrollment numbers, partnership status, and technical hurdles in the learning platforms.
02

Quarterly Impact Assessments

Aligned with Quarterly KPIs
  • Formal Evaluation. Every three months, a formal evaluation compares actual outputs against the KPIs — partnerships signed, learners enrolled, modules launched.
03

Participant Feedback Loops

Aligned with Result 3 — Satisfaction
  • Module Surveys. Surveys after each training module measure satisfaction and confidence (the KPI behind Result 3) and feed directly into curriculum updates.
04

Annual Stakeholder Review

Aligned with Strategic Review
  • Year-End Report. A year-end report is delivered to faculty and industry partners (IBPAP, TESDA) to assess sustainability and nationwide scale-up potential.
Project Outlook

Not Resisting AI — Keeping Filipinos Competitive With It

This is not a project intended to resist automation, but to make sure Filipino BPO workers do not fall behind as a consequence.

By creating a workforce that can compete with, rather than succumb to, automation and AI, the project aims to ensure the Philippines maintains its position as a major global hub for the services industry — while adhering to its philosophy of inclusive and sustainable economic development.

The proposal's headline goals are deliberately scoped to what is achievable and measurable within the project's defined Results:

01

Job Opportunities

Help fill the 800,000 projected job opportunities by 2028 that would otherwise go unfulfilled (IBPAP / Jack Madrid)

02

GDP Protection

Safeguard the BPO sector's contribution of ~8.5% of national GDP

03

Risk Reduction

Lower the 89% automation-exposure risk by reskilling vulnerable workers (ILO, 2025)

04

Skills Gap

Close the gap in which only 10% of BPO applicants currently meet required skills (IBPAP)
