How Our Program Works

A step-by-step journey from traditional BPO roles to AI-powered careers

89% BPO Jobs at Risk
Project Objective

Closing the AI Skills Gap, One Worker at a Time

This project's primary objective is to address the growing issue of job displacement and skills mismatch brought on by AI and automation in the Philippine BPO sector.

Accessible Pathways

Provide BPO workers, students, and entry-level professionals with accessible upskilling and reskilling pathways centered on digital, technical, and human-centered skills.

Adaptability & Resilience

Promote workforce adaptability, sustainable economic growth, and resilience through structured training, career counseling, and partnerships.

Four Key Assumptions

Rising demand for digitally-skilled workers, willingness to engage in retraining, collaboration across sectors, and access to technology.

Methodology

Our Learning Methodology

Designed for working professionals

Blended Learning Approach

Our program combines the flexibility of online learning with the effectiveness of in-person instruction to ensure optimal skill development.

Online Self-Paced Modules

Learn at your own schedule with 24/7 access to video lectures, interactive exercises, and digital resources.

Hands-On Practice

Apply skills to real workplace scenarios — the paper specifies hands-on practice as a core element of the blended approach.

Employer-Led On-the-Job Training

Partner BPO firms host trainees so new skills are applied directly in workplace processes, with mentor guidance.

Recognized Micro-Credentials

Earn industry-recognized certificates and digital badges issued through partnerships with credentialing bodies such as TESDA.

Project Activities

Implementation Activities

Four work-streams operationalize the project — each one aligned with a target Result and its KPIs.

01

Technical Skill Acquisition

Aligned with Result 1 — AI & Digital Literacy
  • Curriculum Development. Collaborate with IT professionals and AI educators on modules in Prompt Engineering, RPA software, and AI-enhanced customer service.
  • Digital Learning Hubs. Stand up online Learning Management Systems plus physical "upskilling kiosks" inside BPO hubs for use during downtime.
  • Assessment & Certification. Run pre-training diagnostics and post-training competency tests to validate the 80% pass-rate target.
02

Industry Alignment & Partnerships

Aligned with Result 2 — Bridging the Skills Gap
  • Stakeholder Consultative Summits. Quarterly meetings with IBPAP leaders, BPO executives, and TESDA representatives so curriculum matches live vacancy requirements.
  • Partnership Formalization (MOA Signing). Draft and sign Memorandums of Agreement with at least 10 BPO firms guaranteeing internships or fast-track interview status for graduates.
  • Real-time Skills Mapping. Monthly surveys of BPO HR teams to update the curriculum dynamically based on shifting demand in AI governance and data analytics.
03

Awareness & Advocacy Campaigns

Aligned with Result 3 — Public Awareness
  • "Future-Proof Your Career" Seminar Series. A roadshow across major BPO hubs — Makati, Ortigas, and Cebu — educating workers on AI–human collaboration.
  • Digital Outreach Campaign. Social media activations on LinkedIn, Facebook, and TikTok featuring testimonials from successfully reskilled BPO workers to hit the 2,000-person reach target.
  • Digital Badge Incentive Program. Shareable badges on social profiles to gamify the learning experience and lift participation rates.
04

Workforce Flexibility & Career Support

Aligned with Result 4 — Long-Term Resilience
  • Soft Skills Integration Workshops. "The Human Advantage" sessions on critical thinking, emotional intelligence, and complex problem-solving — the skills AI cannot easily replicate.
  • Post-Training Mentorship. Six-month coaching pairings between program graduates and senior BPO leaders to support retention and upward mobility.
  • Employment Tracking & Longitudinal Study. A database that monitors participant employment status every six months so the project can adjust based on real career progression data.
